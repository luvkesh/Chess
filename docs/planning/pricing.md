## Reasoning

- **Positioning.** Premium includes the coach, every lesson, reviews and all cosmetics, so it sits between Chess.com Gold and Diamond. That makes it worth about $5 to $8 a month in the US.
- **Regional pricing.** Indian app-store prices are usually a third to a half of US prices, so ₹249 a month sits in the usual band of ₹199 to ₹299.
- **Yearly is the main offer.** Parents mostly buy yearly. The yearly plan costs about two thirds of twelve months, which matches Chess.com and Chessable.
- **Season Pass.** It costs a little less than a BGMI Elite Pass, and Premium includes it. To avoid fear-of-missing-out pressure on children, pass owners keep access to a past season's rewards after it ends.
- **Lifetime.** ₹5,999 is kept as a limited founders' offer. It is not shown with a countdown or a "deal ends" message.

## Children

Chackmate's main audience is children, so every price is shown behind a parent gate. There are no loot boxes, no premium currency, no countdown timers and no "only X left" messages. The parent sees the full price and the renewal terms before paying.
