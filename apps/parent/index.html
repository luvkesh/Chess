Your child would like to play online

Chacklate Chess works offline without any of this. Online play, saving progress to an account, reminders and purchases need your permission first.

What we keep

  • A player name we generate (like BraveOtter42). Children cannot type their own, so it never holds a real name.
  • A password, stored as a salted hash.
  • Birth month and year, to know which rules apply. Never the day.
  • Games, ratings and lesson progress.
  • Your email address, so you can sign in here. We do not ask for your child's email.

What we never do

  • No advertising, no ad tracking, no third-party analytics.
  • No chat of any kind. Players cannot message each other.
  • No selling or sharing of data. No location, contacts, camera or microphone.
  • Children only meet other young players online. Friends join by a short code you can see.

Full details are in the privacy policy.
