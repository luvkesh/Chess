The coach

Feedback that sounds like a teacher, not a number.

The coach runs our own chess engine on your device, so it works offline and never needs credits. It spots forks, pins, hanging pieces and missed mates, and turns them into one clear sentence. After the game, an honest review shows the three moments that mattered.

12. Nd5
Best move

Your knight forks the queen and the rook on c7. Black can only save one.

18. Bxh7+?
Mistake

The bishop sacrifice does not work here: after Kxh7 you have no follow-up check. Rook to e1 kept the pressure.

24. Qd2??
Blunder

This leaves mate in one: Rook to e1. Look for checks against your king before every move.

Questions

Good to know

Does the coach need an internet connection?

No. The engine and coach run on your device, so lessons, puzzles and games against the computer work offline. Online play and leaderboards need a connection.

Is it safe for my child?

Chacklate Chess is built for children. Under-18s get a generated player name, are matched only with other young players, and cannot chat. Every purchase needs your PIN, and from the parent page you can set a daily time limit, see their activity, download their data or delete the account.

Which devices are supported?

The browser version runs today on any modern browser. Native apps for iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac and Windows are being built and will share your progress through your account.

How are online ratings calculated?

With Glicko-2, separately for bullet, blitz and rapid. Your rating shows a question mark until you have played five rated games in that pool.

What happens if I disconnect during an online game?

Your game waits for you for 60 seconds while your clock keeps running. Reopen the game and you are put straight back in.

How do you handle cheating and abuse?

Report an opponent from the result screen. Our moderators review reports, and every moderation action is written to a tamper-evident audit log.
