Chackmate is made by Chacklate Experiences.

Chacklate Experiences makes games the way a good chocolatier makes chocolate: with patience, care, and the finest ingredients.

Chackmate began with a simple wish: to give every child a real chess table, with the click of the pieces, a coach who teaches, and friends who play fair.

So we built a premium club. From the Pyramids and the Taj Mahal to Times Square, we set every game in a wonder of the world, created @@COACH@@, a coach who guides you along the way, and made every piece and board beautiful, because a game you love should feel precious.
