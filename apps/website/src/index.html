The Journey Through Time

Eight eras. Eight friends. Eight sets to win.

Each era is a little adventure with a story, a lesson, a few puzzles and a friendly champion to beat. The first four eras are free.

How an era plays

Story first, then the board.

A story

The era's coach tells a short, true tale of the place and its games, then asks a quick question to check you were listening.

A lesson

A spoken lesson on the board: the knight's jump in Egypt, counting captures in Athens, the opposition in Saint Petersburg.

Puzzles

Three puzzles on the same idea, picked for your level, so the new skill sticks.

The champion

Play the coach in their own room, on their own board. Win, and the era's chess set is yours to keep and play with.

Rooms and music

Every era has its own room and its own song.

Candles flicker, the view outside changes with the age, and the music is played on an instrument from that time and place.

The coach

A teacher who explains, not a score.

The coach runs our own chess engine right on your device, so it works offline and needs no credits. It spots forks, pins, loose pieces and missed mates, and turns them into one clear, kind sentence. After each game it shows the three moments that mattered.

For parents

Made for children, with you in charge.

Chacklate Chess is built for children first. These are not settings you have to find; they are how the game works.

You say yes first

Children play offline straight away. Online games, saving to an account, reminders and purchases wait for a parent's permission by email.

No chat with strangers

Players cannot message each other, only send friendly preset reactions. Names are made up for them, so real names never appear, and children only meet other children.

No ads, no tracking

There are no adverts, no third-party analytics and no advertising IDs, in the game or on this website.

Healthy play

Set a daily time limit. There are no countdowns or pressure to keep playing, and a streak forgives a missed day.

Purchases need your PIN

Every purchase and every link out of the game sits behind a parent check. Nothing for sale is random, and nothing bought makes a player stronger.

Questions

Good to know

Who is Chacklate Chess for?

Children first. The stories and lessons are written for young readers and read aloud, so early readers can play too, and the training goes deep enough for teenagers and adults.

Does my child need to know chess already?

No. Praline the Red Panda starts with the board and how each piece moves, and the Journey teaches one new idea in every era.

Is it safe for my child?

Yes, that is the starting point. Children get a made-up player name, meet only other young players, and cannot chat. Every purchase needs your PIN. From the parent page you can set a daily time limit, see their activity, download their data or delete the account.

Does it need the internet?

No. The engine and coach run on the device, so the Journey, lessons, puzzles and games against the animal friends all work offline. Online games and leaderboards need a connection and a parent's permission.

Are the history stories true?

Yes. Each era's story is about a real place and time: Tutankhamun's Senet boards, the Taj Mahal being built, the Murano glassmakers. Chess itself began in India as chaturanga, which the Mughal era tells.

Which devices does it work on?

Any modern browser on a phone, tablet or computer. Apps for iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac and Windows are being made and will share progress through a family account.
